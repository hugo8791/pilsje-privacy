🍺 Pilsje Stream Title

Pilsje Stream Title is a small personal tool that runs on my own PC while I stream. When I start a game, it sets the title (and the Twitch category) of my own live stream on Twitch and YouTube. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026.

  • What it accesses: with the YouTube permission it reads which of my broadcasts is live and the title, description, tags and category of that broadcast, and updates its title and category. With the Twitch permission it updates my channel's title and category.
  • What it stores: only the login tokens, in a file on my own PC. No other data is stored.
  • What it shares: nothing. Data goes only between my PC and the Twitch and YouTube APIs. There is no server, no analytics and no third party.
  • Removing access: revoke it any time in your Google account permissions or Twitch connections, and delete the token file.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
